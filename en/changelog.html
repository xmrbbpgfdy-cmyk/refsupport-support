What’s new

New features, improvements and fixes, with the latest version first. Dates refer to the documented version milestones.

Version 1.8.0

18 October 2026 · Build 7

  • “Aus Liga importieren” (Import from league) finds all competitions on handball.net for the current season: 937 in 2026/27, from the Bundesliga down to the Oberliga, Verbandsliga and Regionsliga competitions of all state associations. Search, filter by association, source, age group and gender, and select via association/district → league → division.
  • “Meine Ligen” (My leagues) shows your starred leagues and individual divisions at the top. They open the fixture list directly; for a whole league with several divisions, choose a division first.
  • Leagues of Handballverband Niedersachsen-Bremen (HVNB) and its regions come directly from nuLiga, including arena addresses and referee appointments. Divisions such as “St. 1/2/3” are grouped into one league.
  • Under “Quellen verwalten” (Manage sources), choose which nuLiga regions appear: HR Braunschweig/Göttingen, HR Hannover-Weser-Leine, HR Bremen-Nordsee, HR Lüneburg-Stade and HR West Niedersachsen. HVNB is enabled by default; the regions are disabled.
  • With “Liga per Link hinzufügen” (Add league by link), paste the address of a league page from handball.net or a nuLiga group page.
  • The season is determined automatically, with the new season starting on 1 July. A match listed in both sources is no longer created twice.
  • “Ändern” (Change) takes you back exactly one step during import.

Version 1.7.9

18 October 2026

  • When exporting an analysis as PDF or Excel, save the file or share it directly, for example via Mail, Messages, AirDrop or WhatsApp. This works on iPad and Mac.
  • The Excel export now includes your Apple Pencil drawings and handwritten scene notes as images, just like the PDF report.

Version 1.7.8

17 October 2026

  • In delegate mode, the entire timeout display on the team card has a contrast border, making available timeouts and the per-game and per-half allowances easier to see.
  • The notes field no longer freezes when switching repeatedly between keyboard and Apple Pencil.

Version 1.7.7

17 October 2026

  • On Mac, analysis with the video in a separate window gives scene details more room. A compact control bar shows scene markers on the timeline, alongside the scene list and its filters. Drag the divider to adjust the list width; your setting is saved on each device.
  • On iPad, move the video to an external display connected by cable or AirPlay. Keep using the analysis on your iPad; the video returns when the display disconnects.
  • For analysis exports, choose PDF or Excel (.xlsx) and filter scenes by favourites, categories and rating independently of the analysis view. Excel includes “Videozeiten” (Video times), “Chronologisch” (Chronological) and “Nach Bereichen” (By category); the first sheet groups video times by category and rating.
  • Set the match duration to two halves of 30, 25 or 20 minutes, or choose your own half length, and set the length of extra-time periods for each match. The setting locks after throw-off.
  • In analysis, set and fine-tune half-time start markers at the current video position. Individual scenes can also have their own video position.
  • Team time-out entries show the restart, passive play and passes directly in the coach and delegate logs.
  • Contrast borders make white and black team colours easier to see. Favourite stars have a consistent size; on small iPads in portrait orientation, a compact entry button sits beside the note field in coach mode.
  • RefSupport Pro is available as a yearly subscription. Manage your subscription and restore purchases under “My Data”.

Version 1.7.5

8 October 2026

  • Start a team time-out with one tap: the match clock stops immediately and the 60-second countdown begins. Record the restart, passive play and passes directly in the time-out window.
  • Time-out and half-time windows show active suspensions and cards; in delegate mode, half-time also shows which team has the throw-off for the second half.
  • A stopped match clock pulses orange. After the third entry while the clock is stopped, a reminder prompts you to restart it.
  • Match logs show the newest entries first. The match overview is sorted by date and time.
  • Rosters are easier to edit; record suspensions directly for a player, with consecutive suspensions correctly added even after the third-suspension confirmation.
  • A match-start window brings together the throw-off, bench side, team colours and entrance times. After an update, the app shows what has changed when you open it.

Version 1.7.4

28 September 2026

  • On Mac, open the analysis video in a separate window on a second display.
  • Set the half-time interval for each match. A reminder helps you restart a stopped match clock.
  • Analysis mode displays the number of scenes. Expense claims use more descriptive download names.
  • Consistent buttons make the different views easier to use.

Version 1.7.3

27 September 2026

  • Import fixtures from the men’s and women’s first and second Bundesligas and both DHB Cups, with matchday lists, search, venue addresses and all appointed officials where supplied by the source.
  • Create a match manually, from a league or from a shared match file, and review it before importing. Previously imported fixtures are recognised, and downloaded fixtures remain available offline.
  • A buzzer guide walks delegates through the decision and records the result in the match log. Hide individual match situations in settings.
  • Type comments for analysis scenes. Corrections and system entries are hidden in analysis and analysis reports; handwritten comments can be deleted after confirmation.
  • More consistent colours in light and dark mode, with consistent delete confirmations.

Version 1.7.2

17 September 2026

  • Adjust or mute video volume in analysis mode. The setting is remembered for the next video.
  • Match situations in delegate mode bring together video review, extra time and seven-metre shoot-outs. A video review is logged when you tick the first checklist item.
  • During extra time, the match clock continues beyond 60:00. Match periods and the interval countdown stay visible, with automatic stops at the end of each period.
  • Choose shoot-out takers from the roster, log attempts and export the result. Disqualified players remain visible and cannot be selected.

Version 1.7.1

17 September 2026

  • Deleted matches stay deleted after the next iCloud sync.
  • Match and document sync is more reliable, with clearer messages when iCloud encounters a problem.
  • Matches missing from the device can be restored from iCloud. Additional backups help protect the match list against data loss.

Version 1.7

15 September 2026

  • Add a general note for each match alongside individual analysis scenes, including handwriting on iPad. It appears in the analysis report and PDF.
  • On Mac, the video keeps its size when you select a scene, with longer scene details scrolling alongside it.
  • Use the arrow keys on Mac to change playback speed, skip five seconds or step through individual frames while paused.

Version 1.6.6

15 September 2026

  • In analysis-only mode, create scenes immediately without choosing a team. Assign or remove a team later.
  • Add drawings to analysis scenes on iPad, with annotations included in the report.
  • On Mac, press the space bar to play or pause video when no text field is active.

Version 1.6.5

11 September 2026

  • Analysis reports and PDF exports combine entries made on iPad with reviewed video scenes.
  • Open and export analyses directly from the match overview.
  • New scenes are selected for editing immediately. A timeline slider makes it easier to navigate between video time and match time.
  • Switch halves explicitly with a button. Time corrections take the selected reason into account and preserve remaining suspension times when aligning the clock.

Version 1.6.4

1 September 2026

  • Add your own questions to delegate checklists and reuse them in new matches.
  • Correct suspensions more precisely and record them consecutively or in parallel. Prompts help with third suspensions and written-report requirements.
  • Roster entry also supports team official E.
  • Choose to show past fixtures when importing from a league.

Version 1.6.2

31 August 2026

Earliest documented version; the list describes the features available at that point.

  • Prepare matches, create them manually or import from supported leagues, and manage them in the archive.
  • Record match time, scores, sanctions, time-outs and observations in coaching and delegate modes.
  • Review video scenes, keep your documents at hand and sync matches between your devices using iCloud.
  • Export match logs and expense claims as PDFs, and share matches as files.
